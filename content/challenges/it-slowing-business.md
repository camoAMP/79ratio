# IT Is Slowing the Business Down

## Entry

### Headline
When IT slows the business down, something fundamental is misaligned.

### Sub-headline
Not because people aren't working hard — but because systems weren't designed for today's operations.

---

## What This Looks Like

When IT becomes a bottleneck, it looks like friction:

- Simple requests take too long
- Teams build workarounds
- Decisions require too many meetings
- Changes feel risky
- IT is always "busy" but momentum is low

Nothing is broken — but everything feels heavier.

---

## The Real Cause

### Headline
IT slows businesses down when systems evolve without intention.

As organizations grow, technology expands reactively — tools layered on under pressure, integrations patched in, processes adjusted to "make it work." Over time, the system becomes optimized for preventing problems rather than enabling possibilities.

---

## Why This Matters

- Opportunities take longer to act on
- Teams hesitate to improve processes
- Leadership avoids disruption
- Innovation becomes exhausting
- Technology limits strategic thinking

---

## The 79 Ratio Perspective

Technology should accelerate the business — not constrain it.

IT is an operational system, not a support function. When designed intentionally, it enables teams to move faster, make clearer decisions, and act with confidence. When designed reactively, it becomes friction that slows everything down.

---

## What Changes

When technology is intentionally designed for business enablement:

- Faster decisions because information is clear and accessible
- Fewer workarounds because the official way actually works
- Low-risk improvements because systems connect and integrate smoothly
- Systems support momentum instead of create friction
- Leadership regains flexibility to pursue opportunities

---

## Should You Act?

Consider whether this describes your situation:

- Requests feel slow to fulfill
- Reliance on manual workarounds

- Growth creates friction and delays
- Teams would move faster if IT wasn't in the way
