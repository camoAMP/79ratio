# Technology Feels Fragile

## Entry

### Headline
When technology feels fragile, it usually is.

### Sub-headline
Not because it's broken — but because it was never designed to carry today's load.

---

## What This Looks Like

Fragile environments rarely announce themselves loudly. They show up quietly:

- Things work only when certain people watch them
- Small changes create side effects
- Fixes don't increase confidence
- Documentation exists but isn't trusted
- One bad day could cascade

---

## The Real Cause

### Headline
Fragility isn't about bad tools — it's about unmanaged evolution.

Environments grow one decision at a time.
Without a lifecycle model, even good tools create complexity → hidden dependencies → fragility.

---

## Why This Matters

Fragile systems distract:

- Leadership gets pulled into technical decisions
- Teams work around systems
- Risk accumulates quietly
- Growth becomes stressful

---

## The 79 Ratio Perspective

Stability is engineered.

We treat technology as a living system — understanding how it was built, changed, and how it behaves under pressure. We design for clarity, resilience, and intentional evolution rather than reactive patching.

---

## What Changes

System-level fixes lead to:

- Changes no longer feel risky
- Fewer emergencies
- Confidence increases
- Technology fades into the background

---

## Should You Act?

Act if:

- Environment grew quickly
- Tribal knowledge keeps things running
- Fragility increases with growth
- You're hesitant to make changes because impact is unclear
